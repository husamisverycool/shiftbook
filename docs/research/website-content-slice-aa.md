# Website content research — slice aa (2026-10-20)

Research for the public website at tabulado.com: what the category says, what agency owners
complain about, how workflow pages are built, and how a product with no customers earns trust.
[R] = from a source (URL given); [S] = a suggestion. No customers, numbers or quotes are invented.

## 1. How staffing back-office vendors talk

- Bullhorn Pay & Bill (https://www.bullhorn.com/pay-and-bill/): Hero → "The Problem" → "Our
  Solution" → rules engine → reporting → integrated payroll → one quote → demo. [R] "An inaccurate
  paycheck or invoice is the fastest way to lose a client"; pay and bill "often hinges on manual and
  time-consuming validation, repetitive reconciliation, and client-specific tweaks".
- Avionté (https://www.avionte.com/): "front office to back office, in one intelligent platform";
  trust by SOC 2 badge and scale counters. [R]
- TempWorks (https://www.tempworks.com/): "Take back control of your back office processes",
  "Eliminate wasted time caused by manual payroll processes". [R]
- Jombone (https://www.jombone.com/customers/customers-primary-staffing-source/): the closest
  description of the pain — supervisor-signed paper sheets scanned to PDF, "re-keyed into
  QuickBooks Online twice, once for payroll and once for invoicing". [R]
- Words owners and vendors use [R]: weekly payroll, timesheets, re-keyed / double entry, back office,
  pay and bill, invoice disputes, margin, cash flow, "net 30 / net 60" vs paying weekly
  (https://www.reddit.com/r/loansforsmallbusiness/comments/1rg4q39/), compliance, spreadsheets.
- Overused in the category [R pattern, S verdict]: "all-in-one", "end-to-end", "seamless",
  "streamline", "single source of truth", "intelligent platform", "award-winning", counters, logo
  carousels. Nobody shows a pay line explained, names state laws on the home page, or shows the weekly
  sequence — open ground. [S]

## 2. What small agency owners complain about

- Cash gap: weekly payroll vs clients paying in 30–90 days
  (https://www.advancepartners.com/blog/top-7-challenges-facing-staffing-owners/). [R]
- Retyping and rework; disputes when only a weekly total is kept
  (https://everhour.com/time-tracking/timesheet-software-for-staffing-agencies). [R]
- Payroll errors hard to catch; wish for flagging pay below the local minimum
  (https://www.g2.com/products/tempworks-software/reviews?qs=pros-and-cons). [R]
- Incumbent fatigue: "They strong arm you into a long contract", "15 years old technology"
  (https://www.capterra.com/p/76635/Avionte-for-Staffing-Firms/reviews/?page=2). [R]
- Thin teams: vulnerable to one person's absence
  (https://360.techservealliance.org/successful-back-office-strategy/). [R]
- Compliance fear: Illinois 720-hour equal pay, 820 ILCS 175/42
  (https://lrb.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/documents/082001750K42.htm). [R]

## 3. Workflow pages for non-technical buyers

- Gusto, How Gusto works (https://gusto.com/product/how-gusto-works): hero → three benefit tiles →
  "Run payroll in minutes." with a numbered three-step list → one section per module (eyebrow, short
  H2, bullets, screenshot) → FAQ. The review step is explicit. [R]
- Ramp Accounts Payable (https://ramp.com/accounts-payable): hero → "Drag, drop, done." capture →
  code → catch errors → sync, each a three-to-six-word H3 and one concrete sentence → approvals →
  payments → compliance ("No chasing.") → FAQ with pricing → closing CTA. [R]
- Check (https://www.checkhq.com/platform): splits what you do from what Check handles. [R] → Tabulado:
  what Tabulado does / what stays with your payroll provider / what stays with you. [S]

## 4. Trust with no customers

- Mercury security (https://mercury.com/security): short, checkable controls (encryption with extra
  protection for SSNs, two-factor without SMS, yearly penetration test). [R]
- Startup trust pages state compliance status honestly
  (https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/startup-security-trust-page). [R]
- With zero customers: the demo itself is the trust signal; a founder note; no fake proof
  (https://indiehackers.com/post/2c96b9c1c5). [R]
- Tabulado's substitutes for logos [S]: the no-signup demo, the free 720-hour checker, one real pay
  line with its explanation, statute citations per rule, security specifics, "never moves money".

## 5. English and Spanish

- USWDS two-language pattern (https://designsystem.digital.gov/patterns/select-a-language/two-languages/):
  the switch in the header, each language in its own name, to the same page in the other language. [R]
- Square en Español (https://squareup.com/us/en/press/square-available-en-espanol). [R]

## 6. Content decisions for slice aa

Home: hero (the job in the owner's words) → the problem (retyping) → one week, step by step → every
pay line explained → the state rules with their laws → works with your payroll provider (names in
text, no logos) → getting paid → margin → the rest of the back office → what Tabulado does and does
not do → security summary → free Illinois tool → closing CTA. Pricing: one plan, everything included,
the free days, what is not included, an FAQ of owners' real questions (no contract, data export,
provider not switched, Spanish). Security: Mercury-style plain controls plus an honest "not yet"
section. No customers, testimonials, logos or invented numbers anywhere; every number shown comes from
the demo agency and is labelled sample data.
