# Pattern: Website security (tabulado.com/security)

Status: adopted — slice aa, sixth design (2026-10-20; `.claude/plans/website-v6.md`).
Reference product and screen: Linear's security page (dark; a centred eyebrow and large heading with one
phrase decoding into place; sections between hairlines, each a left block — icon, the area in the accent,
a white lead sentence — beside a grid of plain measures), Vercel's security page (the bordered row of
status cells; two-column questions), and the home page's close (Linear). In the site's one dark system.
Source URL: https://linear.app/security
Source URL: https://vercel.com/security
Researched: 2026-10-20 (research report on nav, hero, footer, pricing and security pages). Confidence: high.

## Hierarchy
1. Centred opening on ink with one soft glow: eyebrow pill; the h1 "How we protect your workers'
   information." with the phrase "your workers' information" decoding from scrambled characters once;
   one grey sentence.
2. "Where we are, honestly.": four bordered cells, each a measure and a pill — "In place" (green check)
   or "Not yet" (grey dash). Never overstated.
3. Seven sections (Social Security numbers, before any AI reading, walled off, signing in, the activity
   log, money, the demo), then "Report a problem" when a contact address is set: at the left an icon, the
   area's name in the accent and a white lead sentence; at the right the plain measures, each with a check.
4. Questions (Vercel): heading at the left, accordion at the right.
5. The home page's close; the footer.

## Interactions
- The decode plays once on load; reduced motion shows the words still; screen readers get the words.
- Questions open with the plus turning to a cross.

## States
- No contact address: "Report a problem" is not shown.

Deviations allowed: none
