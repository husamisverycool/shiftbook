0048); the heading is plain white (the sixth design's decoding, coloured phrase is gone).

between hairlines, each a left block — icon, the area's name in grey, a white lead sentence — beside a grid
of plain measures), Vercel's security page (the bordered row of status cells; two-column questions), and
Linear's pre-footer.

## Hierarchy
1. Centred opening: "Security" in 15px grey; the h1 "How we protect your workers' information." (64px,
   white); one 20px grey sentence.
2. "Where we are, honestly." (13px grey): four bordered cells, each a measure (15px) and a pill — "In
   place" (green check) or "Not yet" (grey dash). Never overstated.
3. Seven sections (Social Security numbers, before any AI reading, walled off, signing in, the activity
   log, money, the demo), then "Report a problem" when a contact address is set: at the left an icon, the
   area's name in 15px grey and a 24px white lead sentence; at the right the plain measures in 15px grey,
   each with a check.
4. Questions (Vercel): heading at the left, accordion at the right.
5. Pre-footer (Linear); the footer.
