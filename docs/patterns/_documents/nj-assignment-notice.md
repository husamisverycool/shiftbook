# Pattern: New Jersey Temporary Laborer Assignment Notification (`src/pdf/nj-assignment-notice.tsx`)

Status: adopted — owner, 2026-10-17 ("Finish everything"); SPEC §16, §27.10
Reference: the New Jersey Department of Labor and Workforce Development's own form, MW-23
("Temporary Laborer Assignment Notification | Notificación de Asignación de Trabajadores Temporales,
under | bajo N.J.S.A. 34:8D-3"). The firm must use the form the Commissioner approved (NJDOL's
answer on its Temporary Workers page), so this document reproduces the form's fields, order and
two languages exactly; Granite fills them in. It is the state's form, not a product's design: its
wording is law, not a reference's copy.

## Hierarchy
1. **Top:** the form's title in English, the Spanish title under it; "under | bajo N.J.S.A. 34:8D-3";
   the instruction (give this completed form to each temporary laborer upon assignment; all parties
   keep a copy) in English, then Spanish.
2. **Full name of the temporary laborer.**
3. **Two blocks side by side:** the Temporary Help Service Firm (name, street address, city, state,
   ZIP, phone — the agency's) | the Worksite Employer or Third-Party Client (the client's name and
   address, the phone in the job order's details).
4. **Two blocks side by side:** the firm's workers' compensation carrier (the policy in force on the
   start date that covers New Jersey: carrier and contact, from Compliance settings) | the assigned
   worksite if different from the client's address (the job order's worksite), else "Same as above".
5. **Name and nature of the work** (the job order's position and SOC code); **description of the
   position** (the job order's details); **wages** (the rate card's pay rate on the start date, and
   the overtime rate under New Jersey's rule); **schedule and duration** (daily times; the
   placement's end date, or "not known").
6. **The form's questions**, each answered "No" or "Yes" with what is required, who provides it and
   any cost to the laborer: training; meals; equipment; special clothing; protective equipment;
   licenses; transportation.
7. **Earned Sick Leave** paragraph, in English then Spanish, as the form prints it (the firm attaches
   the Department's Earned Sick Leave notice, as the form says).
8. **Footer:** the date it was made, "Page X of Y".

Every label shows English with Spanish beneath it, as the form does. A worker whose language is
neither English nor Spanish also gets the Department's translated form: that is the separate
"notice in the worker's language" duty.

## Interactions
- Made from its compliance duty (one per designated-classification New Jersey placement) once the
  job order's notice details are filled in; kept as the duty's evidence; download from the duty.

## States
- Details missing (employer's phone, times, description, meals, transportation): the duty asks for
  them before the form can be made.
- No workers' comp policy covering New Jersey on the start date: the carrier block says so, and the
  agency adds the policy in Compliance settings.

Deviations allowed: none
